### Page 2 — What It Is & Target Audience

#### What is Laco Invoice?

Laco Invoice is a dedicated e-commerce finance automation platform that turns raw marketplace order and invoice Excel files into compliant 7% VAT tax invoices, receipts, and ERP-ready accounting vouchers. Simply import your order and tax invoice spreadsheets exported from TikTok Shop, Shopee, Lazada, or your POS. Laco Invoice instantly parses and normalizes the data, validates customer 13-digit Thai Tax IDs, calculates VAT, issues sequentially numbered tax documents, and structures transactions for your ERP chart of accounts. You review with one click, then export single PDFs, bulk ZIP archives, or seamless GL vouchers.

> *"Laco Invoice turns raw marketplace order and tax invoice spreadsheets into ready-to-send 7% VAT tax invoices and audit-ready ERP vouchers — all from one screen."*

#### Who Laco Invoice Is For

- **Solo Sellers & Single Stores (Starter Plan)**  
  You sell on marketplaces and need professional tax invoices whenever buyers request them, without hiring a bookkeeper or manually typing into Excel invoice templates.
- **Growing Multi-Channel Brands (Growth Plan)**  
  You export order sheets from TikTok Shop, Shopee, and Lazada, and your team is overwhelmed trying to consolidate files, verify tax requests, and issue receipts.
- **Multi-Store Sellers, Agencies & Finance Teams (Scale Plan)**  
  You manage multiple store reports and need automated Excel batch imports, bulk PDF ZIP generation, and pre-balanced GL voucher exports into Express ERP or SAP.

#### The Problem

Every marketplace sale requires customer documents and accounting entries — and today, that's a scramble.

- **Messy, mismatched spreadsheets.** TikTok Shop, Shopee, and Lazada all export orders with different column formats, cryptic fee names, and fragmented customer details.
- **Manual tax invoicing drag.** Customers demand official tax invoices, forcing staff to copy-paste buyer names, addresses, and 13-digit Thai Tax IDs from Excel into Word or invoice templates one by one.
- **Month-end reconciliation chaos.** Payouts, platform commission deductions, shipping subsidies, and refunds make reconciling sales from raw spreadsheets feel like detective work.
- **ERP disconnect & double-entry.** Accounting teams re-key data manually into Express, SAP, or QuickBooks, resulting in delayed book closing and costly audit risks.
