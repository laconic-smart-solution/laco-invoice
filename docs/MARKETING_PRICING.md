# Laco Invoice Marketing and Pricing Guide

## One connected workspace for marketplace orders and tax invoices

Laco Invoice helps growing online businesses turn marketplace orders into clear, professional tax invoices and receipts without the manual chase. Connect your stores, keep orders in one place, issue documents with confidence, and give finance a cleaner path into ERP workflows.

Built for sellers operating across marketplaces such as TikTok Shop, Shopee, Lazada, and Amazon, Laco Invoice brings order sync, document generation, and downloadable records together in one practical workspace.

**Pricing status:** Proposed public pricing for review. All prices are in Thai baht (THB), exclude VAT, and are billed monthly unless stated otherwise.

## Why teams choose Laco Invoice

- **Less manual admin** — Bring marketplace orders into one searchable, filterable view instead of reconciling multiple seller portals.
- **Invoice documents when you need them** — Generate tax invoices and receipts with sequential invoice numbers and 7% VAT applied to each document.
- **Faster month-end work** — Download a single PDF when needed or package multiple documents into one ZIP file for finance, customers, or auditors.
- **A smoother handoff to finance** — Keep invoice records organised and prepare invoice information for ERP voucher workflows.
- **Ready to grow with your store portfolio** — Add stores, manage product and SKU information, and keep operations clear as order volume increases.

## How it works

1. **Connect your store** — Link a marketplace storefront and bring orders into Laco Invoice.
2. **Keep orders current** — Run a sync whenever you need it, with automatic syncing available for a more up-to-date order view.
3. **Issue the document** — Open an order, generate its invoice, and create a tax invoice or receipt PDF.
4. **Share or archive** — Print, download, or export individual documents and bulk ZIP packages.
5. **Keep finance moving** — Use organised invoice records as a clean starting point for ERP voucher processing.
