What is Laco Invoice?

Laco Invoice is a dedicated e-commerce finance automation platform that turns raw marketplace order and invoice Excel files into compliant 7% VAT tax invoices, receipts, and ERP-ready accounting vouchers. Simply import your order and tax invoice spreadsheets exported from TikTok Shop, Shopee, Lazada, or your POS. Laco Invoice instantly parses and normalizes the data, validates customer 13-digit Thai Tax IDs, calculates VAT, issues sequentially numbered tax documents, and structures transactions for your ERP chart of accounts. You review with one click, then export single PDFs, bulk ZIP archives, or seamless GL vouchers.

The Problem

Every marketplace sale requires customer documents and accounting entries — and today, that's a scramble.

  • Messy, mismatched spreadsheets. TikTok Shop, Shopee, and Lazada all export orders with different column formats, cryptic fee names, and fragmented customer details.
  • Manual tax invoicing drag. Customers demand official tax invoices, forcing staff to copy-paste buyer names, addresses, and 13-digit Thai Tax IDs from Excel into Word or templates one by one.
  • Month-end reconciliation chaos. Payout reports, platform commission deductions, shipping subsidies, and refunds make reconciling sales from raw spreadsheets feel like detective work.
  • ERP disconnect & double-entry. Accounting teams re-key data manually into Express, SAP, or QuickBooks, resulting in delayed book closing and costly audit risks.
